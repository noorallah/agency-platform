# Questions for the customer

For the person meeting a distributor before or after a demonstration. It is
the list of what to ask so that the firm can be set up as the business
actually runs, and so that anything the application does not do is found on
the first day and not after the sale.

**How to use it.** Ask section 1 before the demonstration: the answers decide
what to show. Ask the rest after it, when the customer has seen the screens
and the questions make sense. Write the answer in the customer's own words.
Where an answer falls outside the application, write it in section 11 and do
not say "yes" to it.

Each question says what it decides. Where that is a setting, the customer's
answer can be set up the same day; where it says **check**, the answer may be
something the application does not do today.

---

## 1. The business (ask first)

| # | Ask | What it decides |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 | What do you sell? Name each line of goods. | The goods types and categories to set up; which examples to use in the demonstration |
| 1.2 | Is it one business on paper, or several? How many GST numbers? | One firm with several goods types, or a firm per line |
| 1.3 | How many branches, shops and godowns, and in which states? | Branches, warehouses, a GSTIN per branch |
| 1.4 | Who are your customers: retailers, dealers, institutions, walk-in buyers? Roughly how many? | Customer groups; whether counter billing is needed |
| 1.5 | How many bills a day, and how many products in the list? | Whether stages are skipped; how the data is brought over |
| 1.6 | What do you use today: Tally, Marg, Busy, Excel, a book? | The import route, and what the customer will compare us with |
| 1.7 | What are the three things that trouble you most today? | What the demonstration must show, in that order |
