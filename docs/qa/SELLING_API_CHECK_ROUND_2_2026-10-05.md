A second pass after backlog 87 (SG-1 to SG-9) and the selling defects through
#1178. All 87 cases TC-SELL-001 to TC-SELL-087 of
`docs/INDEPENDENT_TEST_CASES.md` were driven against the running backend
(`main`, 1.3.0, http://127.0.0.1:8000) over HTTP, on fixture firms built for
this pass by `scripts/test_fixture.py`. Nothing was read from the database:
every figure below came back from a document GET, the journal, the stock
ledger or a report. The earlier pass is `SELLING_API_CHECK_2026-10-05.md` and
is left as it was.

**Results:** Pass, Fail (a defect, see the defect table), Differs (the case
text is wrong; the corrections are listed at the end), Partly (what was not
driven is said), Not driven.

**What HTTP cannot see:** labels, dialogs, prefills, F8/F9, the scanner, the
file picker, which buttons a role is offered. Those halves are marked
*screen, not driven* and are listed at the end.
