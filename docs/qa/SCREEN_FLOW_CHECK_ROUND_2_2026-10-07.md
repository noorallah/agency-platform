## Established behaviour (the book should say this)

<!-- HAND:ESTABLISHED -->
- Notifications are `SelectableText` snackbars of about four seconds; Approve on a sales order says nothing on success (the list's status is the answer, by design in `sales_document_steps.dart:71`); refusals are toasts.
- The close question is "Close without saving?" with **Keep editing** and **Discard and close**, not "Discard unsaved changes?" / "Discard changes" (SO-029, DN-023).
- Cancel on an order takes no reason (the book says "give the reason"); it says "SO-... cancelled. Its stock is released."
- Cancel of an order with a delivery note on it: "SO-... cannot be cancelled while delivery note DN-... stands against it. Cancel those first, or close the order to stop what is still to come." (SO-025 as the book wants.)
- Approve from a stale list: "Only draft sales orders can be approved." (SO-024.) Hold and Release say "SO-... is on hold." / "SO-... released."
- The Wanted-by date picker starts at the order date, so SO-021 cannot be made on screen: prevention, not a message.
- Quantity 0 or negative on an order line: the box says "Enter the quantity." and the form says "Check the fields marked below." No customer: only "Check the fields marked below."
- A cancelled order that was on hold keeps its hold flag (SO-035: the stale Cancel went through over another user's hold).
- Edit exists only on a Draft and, for Field Sales, only on a document that user raised.
- Warehouse (INVENTORY_MANAGER) has no Sell screen and the server answers 403 for sales orders and delivery notes; the hand-over chain of SO-034 cannot reach WH on screen (matches 01_ROLES R06).
- Sales Manager's PUT of credit settings and sales stages answers 403 (checked over HTTP, settings screens not opened).
